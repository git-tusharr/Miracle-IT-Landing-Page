Curriculum

What you’ll learn, module by module

Built for beginners, and deep enough to take you to real modelling work.

Module 1 · 3 weeks

Python for data science

  • Python basics: variables, loops, functions, classes
  • Data structures, files and error handling
  • NumPy for fast numerical work
  • Jupyter notebooks, Git and GitHub

You can: write clean Python to load and manipulate data.

Module 2 · 4 weeks

Statistics & maths

  • Descriptive statistics and distributions
  • Probability and Bayes’ rule
  • Sampling, confidence intervals and hypothesis testing
  • Correlation, regression and A/B testing
  • Linear algebra essentials for machine learning

You can: tell a real pattern from random noise.

Module 3 · 4 weeks

Data wrangling, SQL & EDA

  • Pandas: cleaning, merging, grouping and reshaping
  • SQL: joins, subqueries, CTEs and window functions
  • Handling missing values and outliers
  • Exploratory data analysis with Matplotlib and Seaborn
  • Communicating findings with clear visuals

You can: turn raw, messy data into an analysis-ready dataset.

Module 4 · 5 weeks

Machine learning

  • Regression, classification and clustering
  • Decision trees, random forests and gradient boosting
  • Feature engineering and scikit-learn pipelines
  • Evaluation: precision, recall, ROC-AUC, cross-validation
  • Hyperparameter tuning and avoiding overfitting

You can: build and honestly evaluate a predictive model.

Module 5 · 4 weeks

Advanced topics & capstone

  • Time-series analysis and forecasting
  • Working with text data: NLP basics
  • An introduction to neural networks
  • Deploying a model with FastAPI and Docker
  • Capstone project, portfolio, resume and interview preparation

You have: a deployed model and a finished portfolio.

FAQs

Questions, answered

Do I need to know coding or advanced maths?
No. The course starts with Python from the very beginning, and the statistics and maths are taught step by step in plain language. School-level maths is enough to start.
How is this different from your Data Analytics and AI & ML courses?
Data Analytics focuses on SQL, Excel and dashboards to report on what has happened. Data Science adds statistics and machine learning to predict what happens next. The AI & Machine Learning course goes deeper into neural networks and generative AI. Many students start with one and continue to another.
Do I need a powerful laptop or a GPU?
No. Classes run on the lab workstations, and for practice at home you can use free cloud notebooks such as Google Colab on an ordinary laptop.
What will I have at the end of the course?
Six finished projects on your GitHub, including a deployed model, plus a Miracle IT course completion certificate and help with your resume and interviews.
Is the course online or in person?
It is taught in person at our M.P. Nagar campus in Bhopal, with morning, afternoon and weekend batches.
What placement help do you provide?
Resume and portfolio review, mock technical and HR interviews, and access to placement drives with Miracle IT’s hiring partners. Placement support is guidance and opportunity, not a guarantee of a job.
Is the counselling visit really free?
Yes. Visit the campus, meet the mentors, see the lab and get a roadmap for your background. There is no obligation to enrol.
