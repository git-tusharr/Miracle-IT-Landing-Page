Learning roadmap

Four steps, in the right order

Each step builds on the one before. You never meet a concept before you have the foundation for it.

  1. 1
    Weeks 1–4

    Python & fundamentals

    Python, data handling and the maths you actually need, explained in plain language.

  2. 2
    Weeks 5–10

    Machine learning concepts

    How models learn, how to train them, and how to tell whether they are any good.

  3. 3
    Weeks 11–14

    Real-world projects

    End-to-end builds on messy data, reviewed by your mentor and published on GitHub.

  4. 4
    Weeks 15–20

    Advanced topics

    Deep learning, computer vision, NLP, generative AI, and deploying a model people can use.

Curriculum

What you’ll learn, module by module

Designed for beginners, and deep enough to take you to real project work and advanced AI.

Module 1 · 4 weeks

Python & fundamentals

  • Python basics: variables, loops, functions, classes
  • Data structures, files and error handling
  • NumPy and Pandas for cleaning and shaping data
  • Charts with Matplotlib and Seaborn
  • Statistics, probability and linear algebra essentials
  • Jupyter notebooks, Git and GitHub

You can: load, clean and explore a real dataset on your own.

Module 2 · 6 weeks

Machine learning concepts

  • Supervised vs unsupervised learning, train/test split, overfitting
  • Regression: linear, polynomial, regularisation
  • Classification: logistic regression, KNN, decision trees, random forests
  • Clustering and dimensionality reduction: K-Means, PCA
  • Evaluation: precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, cross-validation
  • Feature engineering and scikit-learn pipelines

You can: pick, train and evaluate the right model for a problem.

Module 3 · 4 weeks

Real-world projects

  • Four end-to-end projects, from raw CSV to tuned model
  • Cleaning messy, incomplete real-world data
  • Hyperparameter tuning and model comparison
  • Explaining results clearly: notebooks, charts, README files
  • One-on-one mentor code review on every project

You have: four finished projects on your GitHub.

Module 4 · 6 weeks

Advanced topics

  • Neural networks and deep learning with PyTorch
  • Computer vision: CNNs and transfer learning
  • NLP: embeddings and transformers
  • Generative AI: LLM APIs, prompting, RAG with vector search
  • Deploying models with FastAPI and Docker

You have: a deployed AI app with a live link.

FAQs

Questions, answered

Do I need to know coding or advanced maths?
No. The course starts with Python from the very beginning, and the maths is taught alongside the ML topics that need it, in plain language. School-level maths is enough to start.
Do I need a powerful laptop or a GPU?
No. Classes run on the lab workstations, and for practice at home you can use free cloud notebooks such as Google Colab on an ordinary laptop.
What will I have at the end of the course?
Six finished projects on your GitHub, including a deployed AI application, plus a Miracle IT course completion certificate and help with your resume and interviews.
Is the course online or in person?
It is taught in person at our M.P. Nagar campus in Bhopal, with morning, afternoon and weekend batches.
What placement help do you provide?
Resume and portfolio review, mock technical and HR interviews, and access to placement drives with Miracle IT’s hiring partners. Placement support is guidance and opportunity, not a guarantee of a job.
Is the counselling visit really free?
Yes. Visit the campus, meet the mentors, see the lab and get a roadmap for your background. There is no obligation to enrol.
